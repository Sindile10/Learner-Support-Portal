Common Questions

Is Learning Hub free?

+

Yes, creating an account and browsing courses is free. Some advanced certificates may have a fee later.

What is the difference between learner and assessor?

+

Learner takes courses and submits work. Assessor checks the work and gives feedback. You can only be one at a time.

Can I use it on my phone?

+

Yes, the site works fine on mobile. We are also working on an app.
