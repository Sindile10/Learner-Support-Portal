REFLECTION FEEDBANK FROM GROUP MEMBERS


During Month 1, our team worked on planning and designing the Learner Support Portal for SkillsTrack Training Centre. We identified the main problem, created user stories, designed the wireframes, planned the programming life cycle, and created the data model for the system. We also planned features such as registration and login, the learner dashboard, task management, support bookings, and progress tracking.
My contribution to the project included helping with the wireframe and planning the learner and assessor features. we also worked on the login flow, task management requirements, Firebase data structure, pseudocode, and object-oriented design. We discussed our ideas as a team and made changes where necessary to improve the system.
From the feedback received, I learned that the wireframe needed to show the main user flow more clearly and that our programming and OOP planning needed more detail. We used this feedback to improve our design and planning. Overall, Month 1 helped me understand the importance of planning before implementation and improved my understanding of JavaScript, Firebase, OOP, wireframes, and teamwork
